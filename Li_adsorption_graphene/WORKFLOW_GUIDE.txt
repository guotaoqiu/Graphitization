================================================================================
Li ADSORPTION ON DOPED GRAPHENE - COMPLETE WORKFLOW GUIDE
================================================================================

OBJECTIVE:
Calculate formation energies for doped graphene and adsorption energies for
Li atoms on pristine and doped graphene surfaces.

KEY FORMULAS:
1. Formation Energy: Ef = Edoped - Epure + EC - Esub
2. Adsorption Energy: Eads = Etotal - Eslab - ELi

================================================================================
WORKFLOW DIAGRAM
================================================================================

    Pristine Graphene (3x3, 18 C atoms)
                |
                v
    [1] Generate Doped Structures
        - Substitute C014 with dopants (B, N, O, F, Si, P, S, Cl, Br, I, etc.)
                |
                v
    [2] Relax Doped Structures (VASP)
        - ISIF=2, LDIPOL=T, IDIPOL=3, IVDW=11
        - Calculate Edoped and Epure
                |
                v
    [3] Calculate Formation Energies
        - Ef = Edoped - Epure + EC - Esub
                |
                v
    [4] Generate Li Adsorption Sites
        - Top, Hollow, Bridge sites for each substrate
                |
                v
    [5] Submit Adsorption Calculations (VASP)
        - Same VASP parameters as step 2
        - Calculate Etotal
                |
                v
    [6] Calculate Adsorption Energies
        - Eads = Etotal - Eslab - ELi
                |
                v
    [7] Analysis & Visualization
        - Identify strongest Li binding sites
        - Compare pristine vs doped substrates

================================================================================
STEP-BY-STEP EXECUTION
================================================================================

STEP 1: Generate Doped Structures
----------------------------------
cd Li_adsorption_graphene/scripts
python3 01_generate_doped_graphene.py

Output:
  - 01_doped_structures/graphene_pristine.vasp
  - 01_doped_structures/graphene_B_doped.vasp
  - 01_doped_structures/graphene_N_doped.vasp
  - ... (one file per dopant)

Expected: ~13 structures (1 pristine + 12 doped)


STEP 2: Relax Doped Structures
-------------------------------
./02_relax_doped_structures.sh

This submits VASP jobs for all doped structures.

Monitor progress:
  squeue -u $USER

Check specific job:
  cd ../03_calculations/doped_relaxation/graphene_B_doped
  tail -f OUTCAR

Wait until all jobs complete (check for "reached required accuracy" in OUTCAR)


STEP 3: Generate Li Adsorption Sites
-------------------------------------
python3 03_generate_li_adsorption.py

Output:
  - 02_adsorption_structures/graphene_pristine_Li_top.vasp
  - 02_adsorption_structures/graphene_pristine_Li_hollow.vasp
  - 02_adsorption_structures/graphene_pristine_Li_bridge.vasp
  - 02_adsorption_structures/graphene_B_Li_top_dopant.vasp
  - ... (3 sites × 13 substrates)

Expected: ~39 adsorption structures


STEP 4: Submit Adsorption Calculations
---------------------------------------
./04_submit_adsorption_jobs.sh

This submits VASP jobs for all Li adsorption configurations.

Monitor:
  squeue -u $USER

Total jobs: ~39 (3 sites per substrate)

Wait for completion (several hours depending on queue)


STEP 5: Analyze Results
------------------------
python3 05_analyze_results.py

Output:
  - 04_analysis/doped_structures_results.csv
  - 04_analysis/adsorption_results.csv
  - 04_analysis/summary_report.txt
  - 04_analysis/complete_results.json


STEP 6: Review Results
----------------------
cd ../04_analysis
cat summary_report.txt
less adsorption_results.csv

Key findings to look for:
  1. Which dopant has lowest formation energy? (most stable doping)
  2. Which substrate shows strongest Li binding? (most negative Eads)
  3. Does B-doped graphene have stronger Li binding than pristine?
  4. Which adsorption site is preferred (top/hollow/bridge)?

================================================================================
TYPICAL RESULTS INTERPRETATION
================================================================================

Formation Energy (Ef):
  Ef < 0 eV   : Thermodynamically favorable doping
  Ef = 0-2 eV : Moderate energy cost, achievable
  Ef > 2 eV   : High energy cost, difficult to synthesize

Expected ranking: B ≈ N < Si < P ≈ S < O < F < Cl < Br < I

Adsorption Energy (Eads):
  Eads < -1.5 eV : Very strong binding
  Eads = -0.5 to -1.5 eV : Moderate binding (typical for Li on graphene)
  Eads > -0.5 eV : Weak binding

Expected: B-doped > N-doped > Pristine > other dopants

Adsorption Sites:
  - Hollow site usually most stable for pristine graphene
  - Top site over dopant often strongest for doped graphene
  - Bridge site typically intermediate

================================================================================
VASP PARAMETERS EXPLANATION
================================================================================

ISIF=2:
  - Relaxes ionic positions only
  - Keeps lattice vectors fixed
  - Essential for 2D materials (prevents z-direction collapse)

LDIPOL=.TRUE., IDIPOL=3:
  - Dipole correction in z-direction (perpendicular to slab)
  - Corrects for artificial electric field from periodic images
  - Important for accurate energies of charged/polar systems

IVDW=11:
  - DFT-D3 method of Grimme
  - Includes van der Waals dispersion corrections
  - Critical for Li-graphene interactions (weak binding)

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON ISSUES
================================================================================

Issue 1: "No structure files found"
Solution: Ensure previous step completed successfully
          Check directory paths in scripts

Issue 2: "mpjob command not found"
Solution: module load mpvasp
          Or ensure mpvasp is in your PATH

Issue 3: VASP job fails with "ZBRENT: fatal error"
Solution: Reduce POTIM in INCAR (try POTIM=0.1)
          May need softer convergence criteria

Issue 4: "No chemical potential for element X"
Solution: Add element to CHEMICAL_POTENTIALS dict in 05_analyze_results.py
          Calculate single-atom energy with VASP

Issue 5: Li atom far from surface after relaxation
Solution: Initial height may be too high
          Edit ADSORPTION_HEIGHT in 03_generate_li_adsorption.py

Issue 6: Calculations too slow
Solution: Try MODE="fast" in submission scripts
          Reduce ENCUT in VASP if appropriate
          Use fewer cores if communication overhead is high
